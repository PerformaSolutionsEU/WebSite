# Accessibility statement

*Last updated: August 11, 2026.*

## Commitment

The website **performa.example** is designed and developed to be accessible to as many users as possible, including people with visual, auditory, motor and cognitive disabilities.

## Standard of conformance

The site is designed to conform to the W3C **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**.

This includes, among other things:

- Text/background contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** for normal text and **3:1** for large text.
- Full keyboard navigation with **visible focus** on every interactive element.
- Semantic HTML structure with `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<footer>` and a correct heading hierarchy (h1, h2, h3).
- **Alt text** on every informative image; `alt=""` on purely decorative images.
- No content that depends solely on color to be understood.
- Respect for user preferences `prefers-reduced-motion` (animations disabled if requested) and `prefers-color-scheme` (light and dark theme support).
- **Skip link** at the top of every page to jump directly to the main content.
- **ARIA landmarks** correctly attributed to primary sections.
- No JavaScript dependency for content consumption: the site is static and readable without JavaScript.

## Verification methodology

Conformance has been verified via:

- **Automated analysis** with axe DevTools and Lighthouse during development.
- **Manual keyboard navigation testing** across all main pages.
- **Contrast verification** on color tokens in both light and dark mode.
- **Screen reader testing** (NVDA on Windows) on key pages.

## Non-accessible content

At the time of publication:

- The **SVG diagram** on the home page is marked as `role="img"` with a descriptive text label (`aria-label`). The diagram is illustrative: all the information it conveys is also described in the surrounding text and on the [What is ABC](/en/activity-based-costing) page.
- **Embedded third-party content** (Google Fonts) is provided by an external domain and its accessibility depends on the provider.

If you find non-accessible content that you should be able to use, please let us know (see below).

## Notes

This statement is issued voluntarily. The website's owner is not, at the current date, a party required by national transposition of the EAA. Nevertheless, we consider accessibility a principle of product quality and commit to maintaining and improving it over time.
